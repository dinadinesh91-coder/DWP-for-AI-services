## What to do if Copilot shows content you shouldn't see

If Copilot returns legal matter information, client details, or other confidential content that you don't recognize or believe you are not authorized to access, **treat it as a security concern immediately**.

## Steps to take right now

1. **Stop using that Copilot thread** – do not continue the conversation or ask follow-up questions.

2. **Take a screenshot** of the exact prompt you entered and Copilot's full response.

3. **Note the details**:
   - Date and time of the incident
   - Your device name
   - The matter name or client identifier mentioned by Copilot
   - Any file names or document paths shown

4. **Report to IT Service Desk immediately**:
   - Explain what happened
   - Provide the screenshot and the details above
   - Mark it as a **security issue** (do not submit as a routine support ticket)

5. **Do not**:
   - Share the content with colleagues
   - Copy or forward the response
   - Delete the evidence before contacting IT
   - Assume it is just a bug or AI glitch

## What IT will do

When you report this, IT will:
- Open a priority security incident
- Escalate to Security Operations, Legal IT, and Data Protection teams
- Validate your permissions and access history
- Check whether this content should have been available to you
- Investigate the root cause (permission drift, group membership change, system misconfiguration, or user error)
- Provide you with an update once the investigation is complete

## Your data protections remain active

Your account security and data-protection controls are in place while this is investigated. This investigation does not affect your normal access to authorized content.

## Questions?

If you have questions before reporting, contact the IT Service Desk and ask to speak with the Copilot Security team.
