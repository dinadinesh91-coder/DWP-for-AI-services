# RCA: Copilot Potential Unauthorized Matter Access (Floor 6)

Version: v1.0  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Prepared by: DWP Service Desk

## 1) Incident Summary
A Floor 6 team member reported that Copilot showed client matter content she believes she should not be able to see. This was reported during the same Monday morning period as other Floor 6 issues.

## 2) Incident Classification
This is a possible data protection issue and must be treated as a security case until checks prove otherwise.

## 3) Confirmed Facts
1. One user reported that Copilot returned matter content she believes was not allowed for her.
2. Report originated from Floor 6 Legal.
3. Report occurred during the same window as other Floor 6 operational issues.

## 4) Unknowns (to confirm)
1. Exact file and storage location of the returned content.
2. Exact prompt and full Copilot response.
3. What access rights the user had at that time.
4. Whether access came from direct permission, a group, or an incorrect setting.
5. Whether this is a single user case or affects more users.

## 5) Most-Likely Cause (Working, Not Final)
Most likely cause is that content access rights were wider than expected, because Copilot normally follows existing access rules.

## 6) Alternative Causes Under Evaluation
1. Group membership changed after migration and expanded access by mistake.
2. Content protection settings were incomplete and allowed wider visibility.
3. The wrong account or session was active at the time.
4. Similar matter names caused confusion.

## 7) Immediate Actions Taken / Required
1. Save all key evidence: time, prompt, response text, screenshot, and matter reference.
2. Escalate to Security, Legal IT, and Data Protection teams.
3. Check what access rights the user had to the reported matter.
4. Keep this case separate from normal support tickets.

## 8) What Must Not Be Done
1. Do not close as "AI weirdness".
2. Do not treat as a normal app issue.
3. Do not delay security escalation while other issues are being worked.

## 9) Verification Criteria for Resolution
Incident can only be closed when all items below are complete:
1. The exact file and location are confirmed.
2. The user's access at the incident time is confirmed.
3. The real cause is identified and recorded, or clearly disproven with evidence.
4. Any access that is too broad is removed and rechecked.
5. Security stakeholders approve closure.

## 10) Preventive Actions
1. Run regular access reviews for sensitive legal content.
2. Add a post-migration check to confirm group memberships are correct.
3. Check content protection settings before broad Copilot rollout.
4. Require service desk to capture Copilot prompt/response evidence at first contact.

## 11) Current RCA Status
Open - evidence collection and access checks are in progress (to confirm).
