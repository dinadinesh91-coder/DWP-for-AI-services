# End-User Communication: Floor 6 Desktop Shortcuts Issue

Version: v1.0  
Date: 2026-08-14  
Audience: Floor 6 users

We know some Floor 6 users are seeing missing desktop shortcuts after the recent Windows 11 migration and updates. Your files are safe.

What to do now:
1. Restart your laptop.
2. Connect to the internet and keep power connected.
3. Wait up to 10 minutes after sign-in.
4. Check your desktop again.

If your shortcuts are still missing, contact the IT Service Desk and include:
- Your device name
- The time you noticed the issue
- A screenshot of your desktop

What IT is doing:
- Checking profile and desktop path settings on affected devices.
- Applying a targeted fix where needed.
- Confirming recovery before closing each case.

Thank you for your patience. We will continue to share updates while we complete device-by-device checks.
