# L1 Self-Service: Screen Stays Black After Sign-In

Version: v 1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If your screen stays black after you sign in, your files and data are safe. This issue affects access to the screen only. It does not delete your documents and does not cause data loss.

## What you can do now
1. Wait up to 60 seconds after sign-in.
2. If the screen is still black, sign out fully.
3. Close the desktop app or browser window completely.
4. Wait 30 seconds.
5. Open the app or browser again and sign in one more time.
6. If you can work normally, continue your day.

## If the issue happens again
1. Sign out again.
2. Stop retrying many times in a row.
3. Contact the Service Desk and share the details below.

## Details to include in your ticket
- Your full name
- Your work username or email
- The time the issue started
- Whether the black screen cleared by itself, and how long it took
- Whether this happened once or multiple times
- Whether you were using the desktop app or a web browser
- Your device name if known (you can find it in Settings > System > About)
- A screenshot of the black screen (if possible)

## What to expect after you report it
The Service Desk may switch you to a working access route while they fix the issue. They may ask you to try signing in again after they confirm the fix.

Contact the Service Desk if the screen stays black for more than 60 seconds, comes back after you try again, or blocks urgent work.
