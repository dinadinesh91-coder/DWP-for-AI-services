The migration has largely settled in well for staff, and the overall user experience is moving in the right direction. Early disruption around sign-in reliability, account lockouts, file access confidence, and connection stability has eased significantly, and many teams now describe day-to-day work as smoother and more reliable than in the first week. Feedback shows that most users can now get into their workspace, access their files, and work through their normal routine with fewer interruptions.

A small number of important issues remain open and are being actively managed. The floor 3 printing problem is still causing repeated disruption for teams that need quick access to client-facing documents, and a newer issue with large spreadsheet stability is now affecting finance productivity. Both are being treated as priority service items with focused troubleshooting, interim workarounds, and escalation where needed. Leadership should expect continued progress updates at regular checkpoints, with clear communication to staff on what is fixed, what is still in progress, and what to do if they are affected.
