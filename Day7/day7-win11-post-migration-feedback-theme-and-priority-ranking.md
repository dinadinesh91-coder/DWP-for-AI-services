## 2) Top 3 To Act On Today (Severity + Volume Weighted)

### 1. AVD sign-in failures and account lockouts (Count: 7)
Why this ranks here:
- This is the highest-volume Blocker theme and directly prevents users from working at all.
- It combines two hard-stop symptoms (cannot log in and account lockout), increasing both urgency and business disruption.

One sentence to manager:
- "AVD sign-in and lockout issues are today’s top priority because they are full work-stoppers affecting the largest blocker cohort."

### 2. OneDrive missing files and sync trust issues (Count: 4)
Why this ranks here:
- Blocker severity: users believe business files are missing, including urgent meeting and reporting content.
- Although lower volume than printer/cosmetic themes, impact is higher because data-access risk can halt critical work and trigger escalation quickly.

One sentence to manager:
- "OneDrive missing-file reports rank second because perceived data loss is a blocker risk with high business and reputational impact."

### 3. Shared drive access denied (S:/Finance) (Count: 3)
Why this ranks here:
- Blocker severity with explicit finance process interruption (month-end reporting blocked).
- Lower count than some Friction themes, but it outranks them because finance shared-drive denial directly stops regulated business operations.

One sentence to manager:
- "S-drive access failures are third priority since they block finance-critical deliverables despite affecting fewer users than friction issues."

## 3) Why Higher-Count Themes Did Not Rank Above These

- Cosmetic UI changes (Count 9) are Minor impact and do not stop work.
- Printer mapping (Count 6) and VPN instability (Count 4) are Friction: serious productivity drag, but users can often continue via workaround.
- Ranking intentionally prioritizes Blockers over larger-volume Minor/Friction themes.
