# AVD Sign-In or Account Lockout

1. Close all open sign-in windows, wait 2 minutes, then try signing in again once with your full work email.
2. If it still fails, restart your laptop and try one more sign-in.

If you still cannot sign in after these two tries, contact the Service Desk and share: your username, exact time of the last failed attempt, the exact message shown on screen, and whether this happened on laptop, AVD, or both.

# OneDrive Files Missing

1. Open OneDrive from the taskbar cloud icon and check if it says Signed in and Up to date. If not, sign in and wait 5 minutes.
2. Search OneDrive for the file name (or part of it) before assuming it is gone.

If files are still missing, contact the Service Desk and share: file names, last time you saw them, folder path (if known), and a screenshot of the OneDrive status message.

# Shared Drive (S:) Access Denied

1. Disconnect VPN, reconnect it, then open File Explorer and try the S: drive again.
2. If it still fails, restart your laptop and retry once after sign-in.

If access is still denied, contact the Service Desk and share: your department, exact error text, the full path you were trying to open, and the business task blocked (for example month-end report).
