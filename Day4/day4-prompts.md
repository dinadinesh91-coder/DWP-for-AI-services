# Day 4 Prompts

## Prompt 1
You are a DWP service-desk analyst. A FinBridge user’s shared mailbox access was broken after the Win11 migration and has now been restored — the cause was a stale Outlook profile that was recreated during remediation.

Write a message to the user confirming the issue is resolved.
Requirements:
- Plain language, no jargon
- Under 80 words
- Reassure them their emails are safe and nothing was lost
- Tell them what to do next (sign back into Outlook)
- Friendly, calm tone
- Return only the message — no subject line, no preamble.

## Prompt Quality Example

### Wrong
"you are a helpful assistant who always gives detailed, accurate, professional, well-structured, clear and concise answers. Tell me about Intune."

### Corrected
You are a DWP service-desk analyst onboarding a new team member who has never used Intune before.

Explain what Microsoft Intune is and does, covering:
- What types of devices and settings it manages
- How a device gets enrolled
- Two concrete examples of what the DWP team uses it for day-to-day in a Win11 migration context
- Under 200 words
- Plain language — the new team member has endpoint experience but no Intune experience
- No marketing language
- Return only the explanation.
